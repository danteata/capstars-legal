# Privacy Policy for Cap Stars

**Last updated: 4 September 2026**

Cap Stars is published by Digitalix ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Cap Stars
mobile game does with your information.

The short version: **Cap Stars has no user account, no advertising and no analytics.** We
do not ask for your name or your email address, and there is no chat. Playing on your own
sends nothing off your device. Playing **online** connects you to another player through
Unity's servers, and that part is described in full below.

---

## What is stored on your device

Cap Stars saves your own progress and preferences so the game is set up the way you left
it:

- your career profile: level, experience, caps earned, and your win, draw and loss record
- the match setup you used last: arena, teams, formation, ball, weather, match length,
  CPU difficulty and the match rule toggles
- audio and control preferences, including whether the commentary is on and how you flick

These are written to the app's own private storage on your device using the standard
Android mechanism for application preferences. They stay on your device, they are never
transmitted to us, and they are removed when you uninstall the game.

## Playing on your own

Playing against the CPU, in local two-player, in a tournament or in the challenges sends
nothing anywhere. No network connection is made and no information leaves your phone.

## Playing online

Online play is the only part of Cap Stars that uses the network. When you open the online
mode, the game uses **Unity Gaming Services**, operated by Unity Technologies, to find
your opponent and carry the match between you. Three of Unity's services are involved:

- **Authentication**, to sign you in *anonymously*. You are never asked to create an
  account or to give a name, an email address or a password. Unity issues a player
  identifier that is tied to the app on your device, not to you as a person.
- **Multiplayer Sessions / Lobby**, to hold the room you and your opponent join. The room
  code you type or share is a short code issued by that service for the room. It is not
  derived from anything about you.
- **Relay**, to pass the match between the two phones. A relay is used instead of a direct
  connection because two phones on mobile networks usually cannot reach each other
  directly.

While an online match is running, the two devices exchange what is needed to keep the same
game on both screens: the positions of the caps and the ball, each flick as it is taken,
the score and the clock, and the kit each player picked. This is game state, not personal
information, and it is not stored after the match ends.

Because a network connection is being made, Unity's servers necessarily see your device's
**IP address**, as any internet server does. Unity acts as our processor for these
services and handles that data under its own terms. Unity publishes its privacy policy at
<https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy>.

Traffic between your device and Unity's relay is encrypted in transit.

## What we do not collect

Cap Stars does not collect, sell or share personal data. Specifically, there is:

- no registration, sign-in, name, email address or password
- no chat, no messaging, and no way to send text to another player — the only thing you
  can type in the game is an alphanumeric room code
- no advertising, no advertising identifier and no advertising network
- no analytics service and no crash-reporting service
- no access to your contacts, your photos, your files, your microphone, your camera or
  your location
- no in-app purchases and no payment information

Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, Unity Purchasing, Unity Cloud Diagnostics and Unity
Performance Reporting are all switched off in this app.

## Permissions

Cap Stars requests internet access, which Android grants without prompting you. It is used
only for online play. The game requests no permission that Android asks you to approve.

## Children

Cap Stars contains no advertising, no purchases and no chat. Online play is limited to a
match against one other person who has your room code, with no way to exchange text or
media.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has
provided us with personal information, please write to us at the address below and we will
act on it.

## Keeping your data, and removing it

Nothing about your gameplay is stored on our servers, because we do not run any. Your
career and settings live on your device: **uninstalling Cap Stars removes them**, and
there is no copy anywhere else to restore.

The anonymous player identifier issued by Unity when you play online is held by Unity. If
you want that record removed, write to us at the address below and we will make the
request on your behalf.

## Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that changes this policy, we will update this page and move
the date at the top. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.
