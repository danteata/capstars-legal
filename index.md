# Cap Stars

The counters ball game: bottle caps flicked across a tabletop pitch, the way it is played
on Ghanaian floors and desks. Flick, pass, score.

Published by Digitalix.

Cap Stars has no account, no advertising and no analytics. Played on your own, it sends
nothing anywhere — your career and settings are saved on your device.

Playing **online** is the exception, and the only one: to connect two phones the game
signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services and passes the match between players
through Unity's relay servers. No name, no email and no chat are involved. The
[privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) sets out exactly what that means.
