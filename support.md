## Reporting a problem

The more of this you can tell us, the faster it gets fixed:

- your device model and Android version
- the version of Cap Stars, from Android Settings > Apps > Cap Stars
- what you were doing when it went wrong, and what you expected instead
- for an online fault: whether you were the one who made the room or the one who joined it

## Common questions

**My flick does not go where I aim.**
Match rules has a **Flick** setting: *pull back* drags away from the cap like a catapult,
*point* flicks toward where you touch. They feel completely different — try the other one.
**Auto set-piece aim** also decides whether corners and free kicks are placed for you.

**The CPU is too hard, or too easy.**
CPU skill runs from Easy to Legend on the match setup screen, next to the formation.

**Online will not connect.**
Both players need a working internet connection. The room code is six characters; case
does not matter and spaces or dashes are ignored, so you cannot get it wrong by typing it
untidily. A code belongs to a room that is open, so once the person who made it has left,
they need to make a new one. If it still fails, one of the two networks is usually blocking
the connection, and a different Wi-Fi or mobile data often fixes it.

**Can I play with someone who is not next to me?**
Yes. Share the room code however you like — the game does not send it for you.

**I have lost my career progress.**
Cap Stars saves your career on your own device only. Clearing the app's data, uninstalling
it, or moving to a new phone loses it, and there is no copy on a server to restore. See the
[privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

**How do I delete my data?**
Uninstalling Cap Stars removes your career, your settings and everything else the game
saved on your device. If you have played online and want the anonymous player record that
Unity holds removed as well, write to us and we will make that request.

## Accessibility

Commentary can be switched off, and so can the sound entirely. The game plays in both
portrait and landscape, and the whole interface re-lays itself to match. If something about
the game makes it hard for you to play, please write and say so — it is useful to know.
