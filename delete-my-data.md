# Delete my data

**Cap Stars** (`com.digitalix.capstars`), published by **Digitalix**.

**Last updated: 19 September 2026**

---

## How to ask for your data to be deleted

1. Email **digitalix@gmail.com** with the subject **Delete my data**.
2. Include your **player ID**. Cap Stars shows it in **Settings**, where it can be copied
   to the clipboard. The ID is the only thing that identifies your record — there is no
   name, email address or account to look you up by — so a request without it cannot be
   matched to anything.
3. We reply to confirm, and complete the deletion **within 30 days**.

There is no charge, and you do not need an account to ask.

---

## What is deleted

**The anonymous player identifier** issued by Unity Gaming Services, and anything held
against it. That identifier is created the first time you open online play. It is tied to
the app on your device rather than to you as a person, and it is the only record of you
that exists anywhere outside your phone.

## What is kept

**Nothing that identifies you.** Cap Stars has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics
and no crash reporting, so there is no profile, no history and no contact record to keep.
We hold no copy of your career, your results or your settings — those never leave your
device.

Temporary room and connection records, created while you are in an online match, are not
kept either: they expire on their own when the match ends.

## What is never collected

Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, location and payment
details. There is no chat or messaging of any kind between players — the only thing you
can type in Cap Stars is an alphanumeric room code.

---

## Your own device

Your career, your medals, the kits you picked and your settings are saved in the app's
private storage on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

- **Uninstalling Cap Stars removes all of it**, and there is no copy elsewhere to restore.
- To clear it without uninstalling: **Settings → Apps → Cap Stars → Storage → Clear data**.

## If you only play offline

Nothing leaves your device, so there is nothing for us to delete.
